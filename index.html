echoes of lost affection

we held hands in the dark, hoping the shadows wouldn't notice, how quickly a flame turns to ash when left out in the cold.
i memorized the cadence of your quiet words, only for them to become a ghost that haunts every silent room i ever walk into.
to love you was like reaching for stars from the bottom of an ocean— beautiful to look at, but silent and out of reach.
now i trace the edges of memories we never got to make, loving someone who exists only in the quiet hours of yesterday.
